For Chart A, avoid startups with:

<10 employees and no product-market fit

chaotic founders

constant pivoting

unclear ML roadmap

“AI hype” with no real deployment

Those environments create instability, which Saturn-heavy charts dislike.

4) What Type of Startup Works Best

If switching, prioritize startups that have:

Real product revenue

Series A/B stage (not just idea stage)

Dedicated ML use cases

Engineering leadership

Defined roadmap

That gives ownership without chaos.



Before switching, verify:

What problem does the startup solve?

Is ML actually central to the product?

Will you own production models or just experiments?

Who leads engineering?

What is the runway (funding)?

If these check out, the move can be career-accelerating.

A simple test before joining

Ask these questions in interviews:

What percentage of projects reach production?

Do engineers talk to clients or only managers?

Are ML models maintained long term?

How many repeat clients exist?

What is the average project duration?
